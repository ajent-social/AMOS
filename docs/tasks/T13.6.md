## Implementation instructions

1. Implement one real frozen provider adapter with timeout/cancellation and request/usage IDs; keep fake providers confined to tests.
2. Reserve budget before attempts and reconcile charged usage after completion with conservative uncertainty handling.
3. Cap retries, total attempts, elapsed time and concurrency; trip a persisted circuit breaker when limits or unknown charge thresholds are reached.
4. Test provider outage, duplicated response, missing usage, timeout after charge and runaway tool loops.
